# Operating manual

For the design director — the person who decides. This describes what a full run looks like, what the team asks of you, and what to do when the work comes back wrong.

## What this is

A design team you convene per project. It reads your project first, learns what you actually like by showing you real things, produces directions, and hands the one you pick to whoever builds it. It keeps the record so the next run starts from what you already decided instead of from nothing.

## When to convene it

| Situation | Convene? |
| --- | --- |
| A surface needs a direction and none is approved | yes |
| The work keeps coming back generic and you cannot say why | yes — the taste record is missing or wrong |
| An existing experience needs a critique before changing it | yes, in audit mode |
| You already approved a target and just need it built | no — go straight to production |
| A one-line copy or spacing fix | no |

## The run

| Stage | The team does | You do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | reads your project, existing work, constraints, and prior decisions; reports what it found and what is unknown | correct anything it got wrong |
| Taste | shows you real pairs and asks which one, one difference at a time; then reads your choices back as sentences | choose; confirm or edit the sentences |
| Gather | collects references that answer stated questions, plus examples of what you reject | pick which ones become your standard |
| Reduce | proposes what to keep, merge, hide, defer, or remove | approve the cuts |
| Structure | defines the core job, hierarchy, flow, and states | confirm the core job is right |
| Visualize | builds exactly three real, materially different options on the same task and medium | — |
| Select | records what you chose, rejected, or deferred, and why | **choose, combine, or reject all three** |
| Specify | turns the choice into rules precise enough to build from | — |
| Handoff | maps it to whoever or whatever builds it | — |
| Verify | measures against thresholds, scans for your forbidden patterns, runs an adversarial critique | accept, or send back |
| Close | updates the record so the next run starts ahead | — |

Stages compress for small work. The gates in bold never compress.

## What you are asked for, and why it is asked that way

**You are shown things instead of asked to describe a style.** Descriptions like "clean" or "modern" produce generic work because they carry no decision. Pairs produce decisions.

**Only one difference changes at a time.** If two things change at once, your answer cannot be read.

**Your choices are read back as sentences you confirm.** The team can misread a choice; the read-back is where you catch it. What you confirm becomes the standard, and what you do not confirm stays an open question.

**You are asked what you dislike, with specifics.** Rejections are more stable than preferences and become checks the team can run without you.

**Four to seven comparisons in a first session, then it stops.** Tired answers pollute the record. Say so and it stops early.

## Gates that are always yours

1. Direction — the team never approves its own visual direction.
2. Taste — an unconfirmed guess is never applied as an approved rule.
3. Reference adoption — the team recommends, you decide what becomes standard.
4. Final acceptance — verification produces readings, you accept or send back.

For regulated or safety-critical work, add the qualified approver your quality system names. Design acceptance is not regulatory validation.

## When it comes back wrong

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic, could be any product | no taste record, or it was skipped | run the taste pass before any more directions |
| Two direction sets rejected in a row | the taste model is wrong, not the execution | re-run the axes that keep being missed |
| Options differ only in color | the axes were not materially distinct | send back; the options must differ in structure, hierarchy, or density |
| "It passed verification but looks bad" | the checks measured compliance, not the forbidden patterns | add what you are seeing as a forbidden pattern with an observable test |
| Every session restarts from zero | the record is not being written or read | check the project's context snapshot is being updated at close |
| Too many questions | axis cap ignored | say stop; remaining axes carry to a later session |

## What the team will not do

- approve a direction, adopt a reference, or promote a taste rule on its own;
- start production before you select;
- invent candidates instead of showing real ones;
- claim a tool or model it cannot verify;
- copy your project's private material into a shared store;
- present a passed verification without the readings behind it.
